Frequently asked questions
Is Saralsetu a government website?
No. Saralsetu is an independent technology platform built by SetuNexa Technologies Private Limited. We are not affiliated with or operated by any government department. We help you discover and apply for government schemes more easily, but final approval always rests with the relevant government authority.
Is checking my eligibility free?
Yes, eligibility checking is completely free for all users. A flat, transparent fee applies only if you choose to proceed with an application through us, and this is always shown to you before you confirm.
Why didn't I qualify for a scheme I expected to?
Our AI matches your answers against each scheme's official eligibility criteria. If a result doesn't match your expectation, it usually means one detail (income band, age, occupation, or district) didn't align with the scheme's requirement. You can restart the eligibility check anytime with updated information.
How long does an application take to process?
Processing times depend entirely on the government department handling your scheme — this is outside our control. We provide live status tracking wherever the scheme integrates with the e-Governance system, so you always know where things stand.
Is my Aadhaar and personal data safe?
Yes. We use encrypted connections, never sell your data, and only use identifiers like Aadhaar or PAN for the specific scheme application you've authorised. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
Can I use Saralsetu without a smartphone?
Yes — Saralsetu works over WhatsApp, which most users already have. You don't need to install a separate app.
How do I delete my data?
Email support@saralsetu.com with "Data Deletion Request" in the subject line, or message "Delete my data" to our WhatsApp assistant. See full instructions on our Data Deletion page.
