Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 30 June 2026

Saralsetu is owned and operated by SetuNexa Technologies Private Limited. This page explains how you can request deletion of your personal data, in accordance with Meta's Platform Terms for apps using Facebook Login, Instagram, or the WhatsApp Business API.

How Saralsetu Uses Meta Platforms

Saralsetu uses the WhatsApp Business API to allow you to interact with our AI assistant via WhatsApp. We do not store your Facebook or Instagram login credentials on our servers, and we do not use Facebook Login for account creation.

If you have interacted with Saralsetu via WhatsApp, the data we hold may include your phone number, the messages you sent us, and any information you shared during your eligibility check or application process.

How to Request Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your data with Saralsetu in any of the following ways:

Option 1 — Email Us (Recommended)

Send an email to support@saralsetu.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request", including:

  • The phone number you used to message our WhatsApp assistant, or
  • The email address / name associated with your Saralsetu account

We will verify your identity and delete your personal data within 30 days, except where we are legally required to retain certain records (e.g., financial or government-linked application records, which Indian law may require us to retain for up to 7 years).

Option 2 — WhatsApp

Message our WhatsApp assistant directly and type: "Delete my data". Our system will log your request and route it to our support team for processing within 30 days.

Option 3 — Remove App Access via Facebook/Meta Settings

If you connected Saralsetu to your Facebook or Instagram account at any point, you can revoke that connection directly:

  1. Open Facebook → go to Settings & Privacy → Settings
  2. Click Apps and Websites
  3. Find Saralsetu in your list of connected apps
  4. Click Remove to disconnect the app from your Facebook account

Direct link (when signed in to Facebook): facebook.com/settings?tab=applications

Removing the app connection on Facebook stops any future data sharing from Meta to Saralsetu, but does not automatically delete data we may already hold. Please also follow Option 1 or 2 above to request full deletion of your data from our systems.

What Happens After a Deletion Request

Once we receive and verify your request, we will:

  1. Delete your personal data (name, contact details, conversational history, uploaded documents) from our active systems
  2. Remove your data from our AI assistant's conversation history
  3. Retain only what is legally required — for example, records of a government scheme application already submitted on your behalf, which may be subject to statutory retention requirements under Indian law

We will confirm completion of your request via email or WhatsApp within 30 days.
