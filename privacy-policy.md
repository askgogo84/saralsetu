## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly

When you use Saralsetu, we may collect:

- **Identity information** — name, date of birth, gender, photograph (where required for a scheme application)
- **Contact information** — phone number, email address, residential address, district/taluk
- **Government identifiers** — Aadhaar number, PAN, ration card number, caste/income/domicile certificate numbers (only where a specific scheme requires it)
- **Financial information** — bank account details, income details, occupation, landholding details (where required for scheme eligibility or disbursement)
- **Family information** — details of dependents or family members, where a scheme eligibility check requires household-level information
- **Conversational data** — the text, voice notes, or WhatsApp messages you send to our AI assistant while describing your situation
- **Documents** — copies of identity, income, caste, residence, or other certificates you upload or share for verification

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

- Device information (browser type, operating system, IP address)
- Usage data (pages visited, time spent, click patterns, language preference)
- Cookies and similar tracking technologies (see Section 8)

### 2.3 Information from Third Parties

- **DigiLocker** — with your explicit consent, we retrieve verified copies of your government-issued documents
- **e-Sign providers** — signature and authentication confirmation for documents you digitally sign
- **Government e-Governance systems** — application status, reference numbers, and processing updates for schemes you apply to through us
- **WhatsApp Business API (Meta)** — message delivery and account metadata when you interact with us via WhatsApp

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

1. Determine your eligibility for government welfare schemes by matching your profile against scheme criteria
2. Pre-fill and submit scheme applications on your behalf, with your explicit consent at each step
3. Retrieve and verify documents via DigiLocker and similar government-authorised systems
4. Track and notify you of your application status
5. Communicate with you regarding your applications, account, or support requests
6. Improve our AI eligibility-matching engine and conversational assistant
7. Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, or unauthorised access
8. Comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations
9. Send service updates, scheme alerts, or reminders (you may opt out of non-essential communications at any time)

We do **not** use your government identifiers (Aadhaar, PAN, etc.) for any purpose beyond scheme eligibility verification and application submission, and we do not sell such data.

---

## 4. Legal Basis for Processing

Where applicable under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and other Indian data protection law, we process your personal data on the following grounds:

- **Consent** — for most data collection, particularly sensitive identifiers like Aadhaar, income, and caste details, we rely on your specific, informed consent given before each scheme application
- **Legitimate use** — for service functionality such as account security, fraud prevention, and platform improvement
- **Legal obligation** — where required to comply with government reporting or regulatory requirements

You may withdraw consent at any time by contacting us at support@saralsetu.com, though this may limit our ability to process pending applications.

---

## 5. How We Share Your Information

We share your information only in the following circumstances:

| Recipient | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Government departments / e-Governance portals** | To submit and track scheme applications you have authorised |
| **DigiLocker / NSDL / UIDAI-authorised systems** | To verify and retrieve your documents, with your consent |
| **Cloud infrastructure providers** (e.g., Supabase, Vercel) | To securely host and process platform data, under data processing agreements |
| **WhatsApp / Meta** | To deliver messages through our WhatsApp assistant |
| **Law enforcement or regulators** | Where legally required, such as in response to a valid court order or government request |

**We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties for marketing purposes.** We do not share your Aadhaar, financial, or scheme-application data with any advertiser.

## 6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to:

- Complete the scheme application(s) you have requested
- Comply with legal, accounting, or regulatory record-keeping requirements (typically up to 7 years for financial/government-linked records)
- Resolve disputes and enforce our agreements

Conversational data with our AI assistant is retained to improve eligibility-matching accuracy but is anonymised/de-identified where possible. You may request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time (see Section 9).

## 7. Data Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organisational safeguards, including:

- Encryption of data in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest
- Role-based access controls limiting internal access to personal data
- Secure cloud infrastructure with regular security reviews
- Multi-factor authentication for administrative access

While we take reasonable measures to protect your information, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Under the DPDP Act, 2023 and applicable Indian law, you have the right to:

- **Access** — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
- **Correction** — request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
- **Erasure** — request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal retention requirements
- **Withdraw consent** — withdraw consent for processing at any time
- **Grievance redressal** — raise a complaint regarding how your data is handled

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at **support@saralsetu.com**. We will respond within 30 days.
